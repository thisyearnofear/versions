## The one-liner

**VERSIONS is where AI-run distribution channels discover, use, and pay for the
music and product placements that fit their content's ethos — free with
attribution, or a flat-fee/CPM sponsor slot — with every use attributed and
settled on Arc.**

## The problem

AI-run distribution channels (YouTube automation, radio-style feeds, and the
rest) need music and product placements that fit their content's ethos — but
today that means scraping royalty-free libraries, negotiating per-track or
per-campaign deals, or settling for mismatched inventory. Meanwhile, music and
product suppliers sit on under-monetized catalogs with no self-serve way to
reach this exploding distribution lane. Both sides are stuck in a human,
per-deal schlep that can't scale to automated channels.

## The solution — one primitive

A **channel ethos → feed → free-or-paid slot → attribution + settlement**
pipeline:

1. **Channel registers** and connects a real distribution surface; we pull real
   subscriber/view numbers via public API — never self-reported.
2. **We embed the channel's ethos** (content description/history) into the same
   vector space as every listing.
3. **Channel browses a feed** of music and product placements ranked by ethos
   fit — the same primitive, two supply catalogs.
4. **Use it free** with attribution, or **buy a paid sponsor slot** (flat fee or
   CPM) via a self-serve flow on Arc.
5. **Every use is attributed and settled** — free uses instrumented as the data
   flywheel, paid slots settled on a flat supplier/channel/platform split.

We sell the **outcome** — matched, attributed, settled placements — not a
similarity API, not a generation tool, not a streaming service.

## Why we win

- **Incumbents can't follow.** Spotify/UMG own on-platform creation; NCS and
  podcast-ad networks are human-run catalogs and campaigns. None builds the
  automated, self-serve, cross-channel attribution + settlement marketplace
  because it cannibalizes their core model.
- **The graph compounds.** Every ethos match, every free use, every paid slot
  adds to a proprietary dataset: attribution graph + verified distribution +
  settlement rail. No one else is assembling this cross-channel,
  cross-listing.
- **Zero marginal cost to match listing N.** Scale favors us; it hurts
  human-run ad-sales and sync teams.
- **Free tier is the wedge, not a loss leader.** Free-with-attribution is the
  adoption wedge and the labeled-data flywheel that makes paid conversion
  provable ("this catalog was used X times this month").

## Market & wedge

**Beachhead:** onboard a first cohort of real AI-run distribution channels and
a first set of music + product listings; prove the full loop from ethos match
to attributed free use to paid slot. Then expand channel-by-channel,
listing-by-listing.

**Expansion:** sell the primitive as an outcome to suppliers, brands, and
distribution tools. Optionally abstract the settlement rail beyond music and
product placement.

## The ask

A first cohort of AI-run distribution channels willing to connect real
distribution surfaces, plus a first set of music and product listings. That's
the pilot. Everything else — the matching, the attribution graph, the
settlement rail — is already running.
